# 🌸 Bloom

A calm, beautiful period tracker that helps you get ready before your period arrives.

## Features
- Cycle ring showing your current phase
- Period, ovulation, and fertile window predictions
- Smart reminders before and after your expected date
- Daily log for flow, mood, symptoms, sleep, and water
- "Be Ready" checklist
- Insights on cycle length and symptom trends
- Guided onboarding for new users
- Dark mode, PIN lock, and discreet mode

## Privacy
No login and no servers. Everything is stored locally in your browser.

## Disclaimer
Predictions are estimates and are not medical advice.
